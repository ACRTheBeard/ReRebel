# Map Baseline: 1.01/1.02 (Prima guide) vs community edition

Baseline source [W]: Prima Official Strategy Guide, Appendix A (Systems and Sectors), via the Internet Archive full-text OCR (https://archive.org/stream/star-wars-rebellion-guide/). 2026-09-08 web research.
Community source [G]: `files/systems.csv` + `files/sectors.csv` (installed v1.02.00 25th Anniversary community edition).

OCR caveats: two original names were lost to non-Latin OCR garbling (counted 198 of 200); a few spellings below may be OCR noise rather than true renames (flagged "?"). Matching is case/apostrophe-insensitive.
